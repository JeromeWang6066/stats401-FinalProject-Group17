What counts as an overtake?

Broadcasts treat overtake count as a race-quality score; OpenF1 rows can be on-track passes, pits, retirements, SC shuffles, or post-flag changes. This project tags swaps and asks how much of the count is a true on-track pass.

5. What share is a pass

One race, one sprint, or a whole season. Each slice is an exclusive tag (shares add to 100%). Across 19,303 rows the large pieces are pit (~46.2%) and on-track (~43.3%). Click a slice to hold that tag on the network and map; changing session here moves the other four views onto that race.
